Loss-Less JPEG

Choose Tools > Loss-Less Jpeg to transform JPEG files without decoding and re-compressing their image data.

Choose no transform, horizontal or vertical mirror, or rotation by 90, 180 or 270 degrees. Optional settings trim incomplete edge blocks, optimise coding, write progressive JPEG data or convert to grey scale.

Camera orientation is folded into the selected transform and normalised. Metadata markers are copied to the completed output. Results go to a destination folder, normally Transformed, and originals remain unchanged.
