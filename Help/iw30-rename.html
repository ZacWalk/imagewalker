Batch Rename

Select files and press F7 or choose Rename. A run of # characters is a zero-padded sequence number; {token} substitutes metadata. Review shows the complete new names, including extensions and companion files.

Invalid names, duplicate destinations and unresolved collisions block Run. Changing the template, start number or collision policy generates a new plan. Cycles are staged through temporary names rather than overwriting a source.

Cancel means partial completion. A failed row is restored only where restoration cannot overwrite another file; earlier successful rows stay renamed. Read the results before retrying.
