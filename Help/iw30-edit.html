Photo Edit

Focus a photo and press F12 or choose Edit. Adjustments change a live preview; the original file is untouched until Save. Use the photo strip to select another image. Leaving a changed draft offers Save, Don't Save and Cancel.

Rotate and straighten correct orientation; colour controls adjust the appearance. Auto straighten looks for strong straight edges, while Auto colour adjusts luminance and white balance. Inspect automatic results before saving.

Save renders at full resolution. In-place Save makes an original backup by default. Save as proposes an unused -edit name. A failed save retains the draft, and a format change requires an explicit destination choice.

Perspective shows four draggable corners on the original photo. Place them clockwise around the document, then inspect Preview. Auto document proposes a complete, contrasting quadrilateral; if it cannot find one it reports that result and leaves the draft intact. You can always adjust the corners manually.

Crop edges and corners resize the crop; dragging inside moves it. Escape cancels an active drag. Draft Undo and Redo restore adjustments without writing the file. Perspective output is bounded to 128 million pixels and four times the source area; unsupported geometry is refused.
