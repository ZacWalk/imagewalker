Photo and Media Edit

Photos

Focus a photo and press E or choose Edit. Adjustments change a live preview; the original file is untouched until Save. Use the photo strip to select another image. Leaving a changed draft offers Save, Don't Save and Cancel.

Rotate and straighten correct orientation; colour controls adjust the appearance. Auto straighten looks for strong straight edges, while Auto colour adjusts luminance and white balance. Inspect automatic results before saving.

Drag the blue comparison handle to see the original on the left and the finished edit on the right. Choose Preview to see them side by side, or Compare for a persistent divider. Escape cancels an active drag first; otherwise it leaves Edit.

The colour histogram updates with the preview. Drag one of the three handles under the graph to adjust Darks, Midtones or Lights; double-click to reset it. Click an adjustment's number to type a value, then press Enter.

Save renders at full resolution and keeps the original beside the edited photo under an unused -original name. Save as proposes an unused -edit name and never replaces another file. A failed save retains the draft.

Photos are edited upright, as the camera recorded them, and Save writes upright pixels. Save and Save as can carry camera details, dates, GPS position, captions and the colour profile when the output format supports them. Orientation is reset so other programs do not turn the photo again. BMP and WebP output do not carry photo metadata or a colour profile.

Photo Edit writes one picture at eight bits per channel. Use Save as to keep the original when editing a multi-page TIFF, an animation, an HDR or higher bit depth image, or a file whose content does not match its extension.

Horizontal and Vertical correct perspective. Corners shows four draggable points on the original photo for more control. Place them clockwise around the document, then inspect Preview. Auto document proposes a complete, contrasting quadrilateral; if it cannot find one it reports that result and leaves the draft intact. You can always adjust the corners manually.

Crop edges and corners resize the crop; dragging inside moves it. Escape cancels an active drag. Draft Undo and Redo restore adjustments without writing the file. Perspective output is bounded to 128 million pixels and four times the source area; unsupported geometry is refused.

Video and audio

Focus a video or audio file and press E. Drag the handles below the preview to set a range, or use I and O to mark the playhead. Space plays or pauses. Packet-copy trims use packet boundaries and may start at an earlier video keyframe, so check the saved range.

Remove sound from a video or turn a supported video in 90-degree steps. Rotation is stored in the MP4 or QuickTime track, not encoded into the picture. The index-first option makes these files ready for progressive playback.

ImageWalker copies compressed packets without re-encoding. Save keeps the original beside the new file. Save As rebuilds the container under a new name, even without edits, and never overwrites an existing file. If a container cannot retain the tracks, the save is refused. You can cancel before publication. The preview uses the same built-in playback decoders as Browse, with installed Windows codecs as a fallback.
