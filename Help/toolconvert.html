Convert Images

Choose Tools > Convert Images to save selected images, or images in the current folder, in another format.

Choose the input scope, output format and its options, then choose a destination folder. Subfolders can be included and are reproduced under the destination. The default destination is a Converted subfolder.

The originals are not changed. If a destination name already exists, ImageWalker chooses a unique output name. Conversion to JPEG is lossy; test quality settings on a small selection before processing a large folder.
