Synchronize

Choose a local folder or collection-folder list and a remote tree, then Analyze. A collection maps each root into Remote\root-name; a single-folder scope maps directly to Remote. Missing roots and overlapping or reparse-point trees are refused rather than silently omitted.

Review the four-column comparison and each copy/delete direction. Analyze does not write. Run revalidates every row before acting, and a destination created or changed after Review is not silently replaced.

Deletion does not use the Recycle Bin. A plan containing deletions requires a counted confirmation. Cancel stops future rows and leaves completed work in place; inspect all results after a partial run.

Recovery copies are secured before deletion or replacement. After closing Sync, Edit > Undo can restore completed changes in the current session. Files changed outside ImageWalker are refused, not overwritten.
