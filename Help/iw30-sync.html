Synchronize

Choose a local folder or collection-folder list and a remote tree, then Analyze. A collection maps each root into Remote\root-name; a single-folder scope maps directly to Remote. Missing roots and overlapping or reparse-point trees are refused rather than silently omitted.

Review the four-column comparison and the directions for copying missing files. Analyze does not write. Run never replaces or removes either side's existing files. Files with different sizes or modified times are flagged and kept on both sides.

Cancel stops future rows and leaves completed copies in place. After closing Sync, Edit > Undo can remove completed copies in the current session if they have not changed.
